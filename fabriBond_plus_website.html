About FabriBond Plus

FabriBond Plus is a professional heat-activated repair paper designed for strong, flexible and long-lasting fabric repairs. It is widely used by garment manufacturers, tailors, uniform suppliers, repair shops and individual users. Whether it’s clothing, bags, shoes or outdoor gear, FabriBond Plus gives you a reliable and neat finish.

⚠ Important Note

FabriBond Plus does not work on wool fabric.

Do not use on woven or loose-weave wool fabrics. It works best on most common fabrics and coated materials.
